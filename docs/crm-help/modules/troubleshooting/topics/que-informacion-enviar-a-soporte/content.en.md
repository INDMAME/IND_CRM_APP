# Information to send to support

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 12.6. -->

- Your corporate username, without your password.

- Active company.

- The module and action you were using.

- The visit, sheet, or ticket identifier, when available.

- Approximate date and time.

- The full text of the error message.

- A screenshot that does not show passwords, access codes, or unnecessary data.
