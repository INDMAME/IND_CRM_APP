# Expense sheet statuses

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 10.2. -->

- Draft: your own sheet can be edited, have lines or tickets added, and be deleted, provided that you have permission.

- Approval requested: the sheet is awaiting the manager's decision. It is normally read-only for its owner.

- Approved: the sheet has been accepted. It is read-only except for authorized reversal actions.

- Rejected: the manager has not accepted it. The owner may have a Move to Draft action to correct it.

- Paid: the payment process has been completed. The sheet is read-only.
