# Owner actions

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 10.3. -->

If self-approval (configured in people management) is not enabled, your own sheet in Draft status provides Request approval. Your own sheet in Approval requested status may provide Undo request. A Rejected sheet may provide Move to Draft.

If self-approval is enabled, your own sheet in Draft status may provide Approve directly. Actions for undoing an approval depend on the role and return the sheet to the previous status shown in the confirmation.
