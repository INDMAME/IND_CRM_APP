# Manager actions

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 10.4. -->

When viewing the sheet of a managed person (subordinate), the manager does not edit its content. In Approval requested status, they can Approve or Reject. In Approved status, they can Undo approval. In Rejected status, they can Undo rejection. These reversals return the sheet to the previous status defined by the system.

Pressing a status action displays a confirmation with a Status comment field. Enter a useful explanation, especially when rejecting or reversing a decision, and confirm only after checking the identifier and owner.

> **ACTION DISABLED:** Status actions may remain disabled if the sheet has no lines and does not have a positive total amount. Add or correct the expenses before requesting or carrying out the status change.

> **ACCOUNTING LOCK:** A Paid sheet or a sheet with an accounting voucher is locked in the web application. Do not try to modify it by deleting related data; contact the responsible department.
