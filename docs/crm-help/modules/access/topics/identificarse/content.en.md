# Sign in

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 4.2. -->

1. On the Welcome screen, press Sign in with Microsoft.

![Screenshot of "Sign in" related to: 1. On the Welcome screen, press Sign in with Microsoft.](../../../../assets/manual-1.5/image2.png)

2. If a list of accounts appears, select your corporate account.

3. If prompted, enter your corporate password and press Next or Sign in.

4. If additional verification is requested, approve the notification in the authentication app or enter the code you received. This step may not appear every time you sign in.

5. Wait for the CRM home page to appear.

> **SECURITY:** Never include a password, verification code, personal telephone number, or confidential information in a screenshot for this manual.
