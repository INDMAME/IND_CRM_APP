# Create a ticket from an expense sheet

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 8.8. -->

1. On an editable sheet, open Quick actions and press New ticket.

2. Choose Use camera to photograph the receipt or Choose image to use a photo already saved on your device.

3. Make sure that the entire ticket is visible, evenly lit, free of glare, and that the text is legible. Confirm the image and wait. The application prepares the file, creates the ticket, and attempts to read its data using AI.

4. When processing is complete, the ticket opens in edit mode. Review the date, currency, merchant or description, amounts, category, and recognized lines. AI helps complete the data, but the user is responsible for checking it.

5. Press Save. The ticket is linked to the sheet only after this save completes successfully. Do not close the screen or go back before saving it and making sure the information is correct.

DO NOT LEAVE: Creating the image does not complete the process by itself when starting from a sheet. The ticket opens for review and must be saved. If you leave without saving, it may remain pending and may not appear linked to the sheet. JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP files up to 50 MB are supported.
