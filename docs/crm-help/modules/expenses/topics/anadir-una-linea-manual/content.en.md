# Add a manual expense line

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 8.6. -->

1. On an editable sheet, open Quick actions and press New line.

2. Enter the expense Date.

3. Select the appropriate Category (in some cases, categories are defined differently in each company).

4. Enter a clear Description.

5. Enter Price and Quantity. Review the calculated amount.

6. Select Expense currency and review Currency amount, Exchange rate, and Reimbursement amount.

7. Complete Project, International, Reimbursable, and any other applicable fields.

8. Press Save and confirm.

REQUIRED FIELDS: Description and category must be completed. Price and Quantity must be greater than zero. For the Km category, the price may be calculated from the configuration set for the company and may appear locked.
