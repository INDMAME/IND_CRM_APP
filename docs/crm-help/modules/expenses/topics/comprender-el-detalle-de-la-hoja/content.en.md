# Understand expense sheet details

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 8.5. -->

The upper section contains the general information: Description, Reimbursement amount, Reimbursement currency, Status, Identifier, Reimbursable, Project, Owner when applicable, and Status comment when present. The lower section displays the expense lines.

In Draft status and with edit permissions, the quick actions button provides New ticket, Link ticket, and New line. These options do not appear when the sheet is read-only.
