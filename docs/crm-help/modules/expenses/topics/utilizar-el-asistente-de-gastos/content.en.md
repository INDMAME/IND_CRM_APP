# Use the Expense Assistant

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 8.3.1. -->

The list may display a button that opens the Expense Assistant in the lower-left section of the form. This assistant analyzes only the sheets loaded in the current query. First apply the filters and wait for loading to finish; then open the assistant.

![Screenshot of "Use the Expense Assistant" related to: The list may display a button that opens the Expense Assistant in the lower-left section of the form.](../../../../assets/manual-1.5/image28.png)

You can press Summary, Analytics, or Anomalies, or type a question about the loaded sheets and press Send.

RESPONSIBLE USE OF AI: The assistant provides analytical help and may make mistakes. Check amounts, users, projects, currencies, and statuses against the original cards. Its response does not approve, reject, or modify sheets and does not replace human review.
