# Open the expense sheet list

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 8.2. -->

1. Open the side menu.

2. Under EXPENSES, press Expense Sheets.

3. Wait for the cards to appear. Each card may display the identifier, description, owner, date, currency, amount, status, project, and accounting voucher when available.
