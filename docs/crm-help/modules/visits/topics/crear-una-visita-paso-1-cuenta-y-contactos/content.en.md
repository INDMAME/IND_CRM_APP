# Create a visit: step 1, account and contacts

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 7.3. -->

1. On the History screen, press the Create button or the round button with the plus sign.

2. In Account, type at least four characters of the name or code you want to find. Then press the magnifying glass, the Enter key, or the arrow in the field.

3. Wait for the results to appear and press the correct account.

4. If you want to associate contacts, open the contacts field and select one or more. Contacts are optional: you can create a visit without selecting any.

5. Press Next.

CHANGING THE ACCOUNT: If you change the account after moving forward, the application clears the contacts and visit information to avoid linking information to the wrong customer. Review the form again.
