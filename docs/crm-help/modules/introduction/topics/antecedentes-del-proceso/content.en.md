# Previous process

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 1.2. -->

The previous workflow was as follows:

- The user creates an expense sheet.

- The user physically prints the expense sheet and attaches the tickets/account statement/invoice as supporting documents.

- The user gives the printed sheet to their approver.

- The approver validates the expense sheet and signs it to indicate approval.

- The administrator collects the approved expense sheets (usually every Friday).

- The administrator checks that everything is in order and makes the payment through the bank's online platform.

- The administrator posts the expense journal to the user's account in Axapta and manages the posting of a purchase invoice journal when necessary.

There are minor variations depending on the type of expense, for example, for expense sheets related to a company card.
