# What you need

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 3.1. -->

- A computer, phone, or tablet authorized by the company through your business email account.

- An Internet connection or access to the relevant corporate network.

- A corporate account with permission to access the CRM.

- An up-to-date version of Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome. A browser is the program used to open web pages.
