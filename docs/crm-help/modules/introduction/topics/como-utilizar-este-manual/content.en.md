# How to use this manual

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 2. -->

The following terms are used in this document:

- Press or click: place the pointer over a button and press the left mouse button once. On a phone or tablet, this means tapping once with your finger.

- Type: press inside an empty field and use the keyboard.

- Select: open a list and choose one of its options.

- Scroll: move the mouse wheel or slide your finger up or down to view content that does not fit on the screen.

- Confirm: accept an action in the window that appears. Cancel closes that window without performing the action.

> **RECOMMENDATION:** Follow the steps in the order shown. If the screen displays a message, read it in full before pressing again. Pressing several times may repeat an operation or make it harder to understand what happened.
