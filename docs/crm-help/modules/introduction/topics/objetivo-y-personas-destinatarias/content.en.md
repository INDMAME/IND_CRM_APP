# Purpose and audience

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 1. -->

This manual explains how to access the CRM web application and how to use its Visits, Expense Sheets, and Tickets features. It is written for people with limited technology experience and also describes basic actions such as opening a browser, pressing a button, typing in a field, or returning to the previous screen.

The available options depend on the user, the selected company, and the assigned permissions. One person may therefore see fewer menus or actions than another without this being an error.

> **IMPORTANT:** Do not test actions using real information if you are unsure of their purpose. In particular, always read confirmation messages before saving, deleting, requesting approval, approving, or rejecting.
