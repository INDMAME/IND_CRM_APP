# Relationship between CRM and Axapta

<!-- Source: CRM App Manual 1.5.docx, section 11.1. -->

Sheets and their lines created in the web application are integrated with Axapta and can be viewed there using the same sheet identifier. Administrative roles continue the relevant accounting and payment tasks in Axapta.

For a regular user, the day-to-day work described in this manual is carried out in the web application. You do not need to open Axapta to create lines, attach tickets, or request approval unless your role and the internal procedure state otherwise.

When accounting processing or payment advances in Axapta, the sheet displays Paid status and becomes read-only in the CRM. If the information does not appear up to date, do not duplicate the sheet: refresh the screen and contact the responsible department if the difference remains.
